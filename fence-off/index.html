      How to play

      1. Take turns. On your turn, tap the gap between two neighbouring posts to build one fence there.
      2. Claim plots. If your fence is the fourth side of a plot, the plot is yours and you take another turn right away.
      3. Watch out. A plot with three fences is a gift: whoever fences the fourth side takes it. Try not to hand your friends a free plot.
      4. Finish. The game ends when every plot is claimed. Most plots wins; ties are shared.
