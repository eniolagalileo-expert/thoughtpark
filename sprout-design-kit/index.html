Sprout: a plant-care companion that feels like a garden journal, not a dashboard.

Sprout tells you which houseplants need water today, remembers each plant's history, and makes adding a new plant as easy as taking a photo. This page shows how the look was decided: references first, then rules, then screens.

02 · Design brief

The board, written as rules anyone can build from

Every value below traces back to a tile above. This is the brief the screens were generated from.

Palette

Moss #3F9D5APrimary action, active tab, progress. One accent only.
Terracotta #C9674ASecondary and destructive-ish actions (skip, remove).
Sunlight #F2C14E"Due today" chips. Never for buttons.
Clay #F3E7D8Chips, selected states, plant thumbnails.
Paper #FAF6EEApp background. Cards are white on paper.
Ink #243127All text. Muted text #6F7A70.

Type

DisplayFredoka 600, 26–52px, line-height 1.05
BodyInter 400/600, 14–15px, line-height 1.5
LabelsInter 700, 11–12px, uppercase, +0.12em tracking
Latin namesInter 500, 12px, muted

Shape and space

Grid8pt. Screen padding 18, section gap 24, card padding 12–16.
RadiusCards 18–22px, chips and buttons full pill, thumbnails 14px.
Borders1px #E4DCCD on cards; shadows only on the phone frame.
Icons24px grid, 1.8 stroke, round caps and joins.

Feel: do and don't

  • Do write like a journal: "Water the fern" not "Task: hydration".
  • Do show history as dated notes, one line each.
  • Don't use gloss, gradients on buttons, or more than one accent on a screen.
  • Don't show charts on the home screen; the home screen is today's list.
